RESERVATIONS

For a guaranteed place at our restaurant in the evening please make a reservation down below by filling in the form. If you don't have a reservation we can not guarantee you a place at a table. Note that we only take reservations for the evening restaurant. In the summer you're allowed to bring your own picnic equipment to have a lunch in our garden as long as you pick up after yourself and bring back all our equipment such as plates, glasses and cutlery. We recommend you to check the weather beforehand if you plan on having a picnic or sit outside.
