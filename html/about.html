About Haraldsson Café

About the café and our staff

Haraldsson is a small café and restaurant in the South of Sweden. The café opened in 1976 as a small family-owned café open during the summers. The cozy café was for a long time known only by the locals of the town, but is today attracting tourists from much further away. With the success of the café it expanded in 2005 with the addition of the restaurant and more of our dedicated and diligent staff. Currently we have a staff of 20 people all contributing with their own knowledge and experience. Together we work to make the café and restaurant a pleasant experience for our guests, as well as always improving the quality of our food and service.


About our food

In our staff we have people working in our garden, growing our vegetables, berries and more. This makes it possible for us to use our own home-grown ingredients. This is huge for the quality of our food and to be more climate-friendly. All of the food we grow in our garden is ecological and of excellent quality. Some of the food that we make and produce ourselves are potatoes, onions, herbs, carrots, beets, berries, apples, grapes, peppers and eggs. The foods that we do not grow ourselves we buy from local farmers as much as we can to support their work. It is important for us to know where our food comes from and to use ingredients of the best quality. Our garden is open to visitors and feel free to ask further questions about our food!
